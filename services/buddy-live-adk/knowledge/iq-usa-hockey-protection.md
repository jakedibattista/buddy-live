# Hockey IQ — USA Hockey Puck Protection

Puck protection is the art of keeping your body between the defender and the puck. USA Hockey ADM coaches emphasize that the best players use their bodies as shields to maintain possession.

## Using your body as a shield

When a defender pressures you, your job isn't to skate away or panic—it's to shield the puck. Put your body directly in their way so they have to reach through you to get the puck, which usually forces them to take a penalty!

* **Butt to defender:** turn your back or hip toward the defender. Keep your knees bent low.
* **Puck on the outside:** keep the puck on your outside circle, as far away from the defender's stick as possible.
* **One-hand reach:** you can use one hand on your stick to slide the puck far out wide while using your opposite arm and shoulder to hold off the defender.

## Escaping pressure

Once you've shielded the puck along the boards, you need to make a quick move to escape. 

* **The tight turn:** skate hard, plant your inside skate, and make a sharp, tight turn away from the defender. 
* **The stop and go:** pretend you are going to stop, let the defender slide past you, and then immediately accelerate forward in the same direction.

## Puck protection checklist

1. **Get low:** bend your knees. A low stance makes you incredibly strong and hard for defenders to push off the puck.
2. **Keep moving:** never stand completely still when protecting the puck. Keep your feet moving in short, active circles along the boards.
3. **Head up:** even when protecting the puck, keep your head up so you can spot an open teammate in the slot and make a quick pass.
