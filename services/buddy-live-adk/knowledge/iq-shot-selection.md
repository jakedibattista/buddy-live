# Hockey IQ — shot selection

When to shoot, when to pass, when to skate. The fastest way to score
more goals isn't a harder shot — it's better decisions.

## Breakaway decisions

A breakaway means you have the puck and only the goalie to beat. The
goalie is either:

- **Way out (challenging):** lots of net showing on the sides. Best
  move is to deke (skate around them) or shoot quick to a corner.
- **Square (in good position):** they're set up, knees bent, blocking
  the middle. Best move is a quick wristshot to a corner — high glove
  or low blocker.
- **Down early (butterfly):** they dropped to their knees too soon.
  Best move is to shoot high — over the shoulder.

**Common scenario:** the goalie is way out and you have the puck on
your forehand. Shoot fast or skate around? **Skate around** — easier
goal with the goalie out of position.

## Catching a pass in the slot

The slot is the area right in front of the net, between the faceoff
circles. Highest-percentage shooting area.

- If a defender is sliding into your shot lane, **shoot quick** before
  they get there.
- If the defender is right on top of you, **take a step** to a better
  angle first.
- If the goalie is moving across (after a pass from one side), **shoot
  immediately** before they're set.

## Pass vs shoot

- **Teammate has a better angle:** pass. A 10-foot-wide-open net beats
  a 1-foot opening.
- **You have a better angle:** shoot. Don't pass up open looks.
- **Both covered:** put a shot on net. Rebound, deflection, or just
  forcing a save creates chaos.

## Quick snap vs windup

- **Quick snap (wristshot):** the goalie can't react if you don't
  telegraph the shot. Use when defenders are close or the goalie is
  scrambling.
- **Big windup (slapshot):** only from distance with time and space.
  Telegraphed shots get blocked or saved.

## Shot location aim points

- **Corners** (4 of them): high glove, high blocker, low glove, low
  blocker. Hardest to save.
- **Five-hole:** between the goalie's legs. Often open when they're
  moving across or going down too early.
- **NEVER aim at the goalie's chest.** That's where the pads are —
  always a save.
