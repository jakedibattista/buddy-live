# Recovery moves — analysis wait and cool-down

Standing stretches and light stick work while a scored rep is analyzing
(~30–90 seconds). Coach Buddy picks **one** 30-second move, calls
`start_warmup_timer`, and may ask one inline Hockey IQ question while
the timer runs.

**Tags:** RECOVERY, OFF-ICE  
**Tool:** `lookup_drill_knowledge("recovery stretch off-ice 30 seconds")`  
or paraphrase from this doc when grounding is offline.

## Shoulder rolls (recovery, 30 seconds)

**Label:** Shoulder rolls  
**Exercise:** big slow shoulder rolls forward then backward  
**Spoken demo (10 and under):** Roll your shoulders in slow circles — forward, then backward — thirty seconds.

Releases tension from gripping the stick during the rep.

## Forearm stretch (recovery, 30 seconds)

**Label:** Forearm stretch  
**Exercise:** extend one arm, pull fingers back gently with the other hand, switch sides  
**Spoken demo (10 and under):** Stick your arm out like a stop sign. Pull your fingers back slow — switch arms — thirty seconds.

Forearms work hard on wrist shots and stickhandling.

## Easy stickhandling (recovery, 30 seconds)

**Label:** Easy stickhandling  
**Exercise:** soft puck or ball taps side to side in front of the body  
**Spoken demo (10 and under):** Tap the puck left and right in front of you — soft hands, no rush — thirty seconds.

Keeps hands awake without another scored rep.

## Hip circles (recovery, 30 seconds)

**Label:** Hip circles  
**Exercise:** hands on hips, slow circles with the hips both directions  
**Spoken demo (10 and under):** Hands on your hips. Make slow circles with your hips — like hula — thirty seconds.

Loosens hips after knee-bend shooting.

## Neck and look-around (recovery, 30 seconds)

**Label:** Look-around stretch  
**Exercise:** slow head turns left and right, gentle chin to chest once each way  
**Spoken demo (10 and under):** Look left slow, look right slow, chin to chest once — thirty seconds.

Counteracts staring at one spot during filming.

## Shake-out reset (recovery, 30 seconds)

**Label:** Shake-out  
**Exercise:** shake arms and legs loose, two slow deep breaths  
**Spoken demo (10 and under):** Shake your arms and legs like you're drying off — two big slow breaths — thirty seconds.

Mental reset before the scorecard lands.

## Wall lean calf stretch (recovery, 30 seconds)

**Label:** Calf stretch  
**Exercise:** hands on wall, one foot back, gentle lean until calf stretches, switch  
**Spoken demo (10 and under):** Hands on the wall. Step one foot back and lean in slow — switch legs — thirty seconds.

Safe static stretch after explosive leg drive.
