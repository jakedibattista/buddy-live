# Hockey IQ — USA Hockey Small-Area Games

USA Hockey ADM emphasizes small-area games because playing in tight spaces makes you think and react faster. Learn how to support your teammate and create quick odd-man advantages.

## Quick support in tight spaces

In a small area, you don't have time to wait for a play to develop. If your teammate gets the puck, you must support them immediately. This means getting open in a lane they can actually see.

* **Two-second rule:** when your teammate wins the puck, you have exactly two seconds to find open space.
* **Get close:** in small spaces, don't run away to the opposite side of the rink. Get close enough for a short, crisp pass.

## Creating the 2-on-1

Even if the game is 3-on-3 or 5-on-5, every hockey play is actually a series of tiny 2-on-1s. The goal in tight spaces is to create a quick 2-on-1 with your teammate to beat a single defender.

* **Give and go:** pass the puck to your teammate, then immediately sprint past your defender to open space. Your teammate passes it right back to you for an easy shot!
* **Overload:** move to the same side of the small zone as your teammate with the puck. This forces the defender to choose who to cover, leaving one of you completely open.

## Tight-space checklist

1. **Short passes:** big windups and long passes get intercepted in small spaces. Keep your passes short, flat, and hard.
2. **Move after passing:** never stand still after you pass the puck! Immediately skate to a new open lane.
3. **Talk to your teammate:** use short words like "slot," "here," or "boards" so your teammate knows where you are without looking.
