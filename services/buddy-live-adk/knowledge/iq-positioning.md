# Hockey IQ — positioning and reads

Where to be when you don't have the puck, and how to read what's
happening on the ice. Most of hockey is played without the puck.

## Support and lanes

- **Support** = give your teammate a passing option. If they have the
  puck, you should be skating somewhere they can pass to.
- **Lane** = the imaginary line between two players. A "shot lane"
  is between you and the net. A "pass lane" is between two teammates.

When a teammate has the puck, ask: am I in a lane? Can they see me?
If not, move.

## 2-on-1

You and a teammate against one defender. The goal is to make the
defender pick — they can't stop you both.

- **You have the puck, defender drops between:** quick pass to your
  open teammate.
- **You have the puck, defender stays on you:** you're free to shoot —
  drive wide for a better angle first.
- **Saucer pass:** lift the puck over the defender's stick to a teammate
  on the back side. Hard skill but unstoppable when it works.

**Common scenario:** 2-on-1, defender slides toward the open teammate
to take away the pass. **Keep shooting** — defender just gave you the
shot lane.

## Backchecking

The opposing team has the puck and is heading toward your net. Your
job: get back and pick up a man.

- Find the open opposing player nearest the puck and stick with them.
- Stay between them and your own net.
- Don't all chase the puck carrier — that leaves teammates open.

## Forechecking

The opposing team has the puck in their end. Your job: pressure them
to turn it over.

- **F1** (closest forward) pressures the puck carrier.
- **F2** supports — reads where the puck might go and cuts off the pass.
- **F3** stays high (defensively) so the other team can't break out
  through the middle.

## Awareness checklist (before every shift)

1. **Score:** are we up, down, or tied?
2. **Time:** how much is left in the period / game?
3. **Personnel:** who's on the ice with you? Who's tired? Who's the
   other team's best player?
4. **Situation:** even strength, power play, penalty kill, empty net?

These four answers change every decision you make on the ice.

## Game-situation cues

- **Up by one late:** play safe — chip pucks out, don't force passes.
- **Down by one late:** every shift matters — get the puck deep, cycle
  for a chance.
- **Tied late:** don't gamble on offense. Don't take penalties.
- **Power play:** move the puck quickly, find the open seam.
- **Penalty kill:** clear the puck whenever you can, block lanes.
