# Hockey-specific off-ice warm-up moves

Stick and shooting-prep moves matched to the session's focus drill
(wristshot, slapshot, or backhand). Each move runs **30 seconds**. Coach
Buddy picks **two** per session via `lookup_warmup_moves(category="hockey",
focus_drill=..., count=2)` after the three general moves.

## Stick wipers (hockey, all drills, 30 seconds)

**Label:** Stick wipers  
**Exercise:** stick out front tapping side to side like windshield wipers  
**Spoken demo (10 and under):** Hold your stick out in front. Tap left, then right, like wiping a windshield — thirty seconds.

Wrist and forearm prep for every shot type.

## Shadow wrist shot (wristshot, 30 seconds)

**Label:** Shadow wrist shot  
**Exercise:** slow pretend wrist shot with knees bent and wrist snap  
**Spoken demo (10 and under):** Pretend you're shooting at the net. Bend your knees and snap your wrists — slow motion — thirty seconds.

Rehearses the wristshot chain before the scored rep.

## Puck pulls (wristshot, 30 seconds)

**Label:** Puck pulls  
**Exercise:** slow puck pulls across the body with stick on the floor  
**Spoken demo (10 and under):** Slide an imaginary puck across your body slow, then pull it back — thirty seconds.

Trains hands and weight shift for quick release.

## Stickhandling in place (wristshot / backhand, 30 seconds)

**Label:** Stickhandling  
**Exercise:** quick stickhandling in place with soft hands  
**Spoken demo (10 and under):** Tap the puck or ball left and right in front of you — soft quick hands — thirty seconds.

Soft hands before catching a pass or releasing.

## Shadow slap shot (slapshot, 30 seconds)

**Label:** Shadow slap shot  
**Exercise:** slow pretend slap shot sweeping stick down toward the floor  
**Spoken demo (10 and under):** Pretend a puck is on the floor. Stick back a little, then sweep down slow — thirty seconds.

Grooves the slapshot sweep without a puck.

## Wind-up practice (slapshot, 30 seconds)

**Label:** Wind-up practice  
**Exercise:** slow slap shot wind-up to waist height without full swing  
**Spoken demo (10 and under):** Bring your stick up to your waist slow, then sweep down like a slap shot — thirty seconds.

Short wind-up reps — not over the head.

## Weight shift (slapshot, 30 seconds)

**Label:** Weight shift  
**Exercise:** shift weight from back foot to front foot with stick on floor  
**Spoken demo (10 and under):** Stick on the floor. Shift your weight from back foot to front foot slow — thirty seconds.

Lower-body power sequence for slap shots.

## Shadow backhand (backhand, 30 seconds)

**Label:** Shadow backhand  
**Exercise:** slow pretend backhand sweeping stick across the body  
**Spoken demo (10 and under):** Pretend the puck is on your back foot side. Sweep your stick across your body slow — thirty seconds.

Cup-and-roll motion before the real rep.

## Cross-body pulls (backhand, 30 seconds)

**Label:** Cross-body pulls  
**Exercise:** slow cross-body puck pulls with stick cupping motion  
**Spoken demo (10 and under):** Pull an imaginary puck across your body slow, cupping with your stick — thirty seconds.

Backhand hand path and blade angle prep.

## Quick feet in place (hockey, all drills, 30 seconds)

**Label:** Quick feet  
**Exercise:** fast small steps in place on the balls of the feet, knees bent  
**Spoken demo (10 and under):** Quick tiny steps in place — like the floor is hot — stay low — thirty seconds.

Wakes up footwork before shooting.

## One-timer shadow (hockey, wristshot / slapshot, 30 seconds)

**Label:** One-timer shadow  
**Exercise:** slow pretend one-timer as if receiving a pass, no windup  
**Spoken demo (10 and under):** Pretend a pass is coming — shoot in one motion, no big windup — thirty seconds.

Trains quick release timing off a pass.
