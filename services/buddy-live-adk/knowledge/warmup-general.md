# Off-ice general warm-up moves

Standing body-loosening moves for youth hockey players before stick work.
Each move runs **30 seconds**. Coach Buddy picks **three** per session from
this list so warm-ups feel fresh — not the same four moves every time.

Use with `lookup_warmup_moves(category="general", count=3)`.

## Arm circles (general, 30 seconds)

**Label:** Arm circles  
**Exercise:** slow arm circles with arms out wide  
**Spoken demo (10 and under):** Spread your arms like airplane wings. Make big slow circles — thirty seconds.

Loosens shoulders and upper back before stick handling.

## High knees (general, 30 seconds)

**Label:** High knees  
**Exercise:** march in place lifting knees high  
**Spoken demo (10 and under):** March in place. Lift each knee up high, like stepping over a puddle — thirty seconds.

Wakes up hip flexors and gets heart rate up without needing ice.

## Torso twists (general, 30 seconds)

**Label:** Torso twists  
**Exercise:** standing torso twists with arms loose at the sides  
**Spoken demo (10 and under):** Stand tall and twist your shoulders left, then right — like you're looking behind you — thirty seconds.

Prepares the core for shooting rotation.

## Leg swings (general, 30 seconds)

**Label:** Leg swings  
**Exercise:** slow forward and backward leg swings holding a wall or chair  
**Spoken demo (10 and under):** Hold something steady. Swing one leg forward and back slow, then switch — thirty seconds.

Opens hips and hamstrings for a balanced stance.

## Shoulder rolls (general, 30 seconds)

**Label:** Shoulder rolls  
**Exercise:** big slow shoulder rolls forward then backward  
**Spoken demo (10 and under):** Roll your shoulders in big slow circles — forward, then backward — thirty seconds.

Releases tension from carrying a stick and school backpacks.

## Standing squats (general, 30 seconds)

**Label:** Standing squats  
**Exercise:** slow bodyweight squats with feet shoulder-width apart  
**Spoken demo (10 and under):** Feet apart, bend your knees like sitting in a chair, then stand up slow — thirty seconds.

Builds the leg drive players need for knee bend in shots.

## Calf raises (general, 30 seconds)

**Label:** Calf raises  
**Exercise:** slow calf raises rising onto toes then lowering heels  
**Spoken demo (10 and under):** Rise up on your toes slow, then lower your heels — like you're reaching for a shelf — thirty seconds.

Activates ankles for balance during the shooting stance.

## Ankle circles (general, 30 seconds)

**Label:** Ankle circles  
**Exercise:** lift one foot slightly, slow circles with the ankle, switch feet  
**Spoken demo (10 and under):** Lift one foot a little. Make slow circles with your ankle — switch feet — thirty seconds.

Ankle mobility for balance in the shooting stance.

## Marching with stick overhead (general, 30 seconds)

**Label:** Overhead march  
**Exercise:** march in place holding stick overhead with straight arms  
**Spoken demo (10 and under):** Hold your stick up high like a tunnel. March in place — thirty seconds.

Opens shoulders and core without jumping.

## Lateral shuffles (general, 30 seconds)

**Label:** Side shuffles  
**Exercise:** slow side-to-side shuffles with knees bent, stay facing forward  
**Spoken demo (10 and under):** Bend your knees. Shuffle left, shuffle right — stay facing forward — thirty seconds.

Hockey-ready lateral movement pattern.
