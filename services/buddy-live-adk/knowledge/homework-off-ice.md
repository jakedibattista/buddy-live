# Off-ice homework drills by weakest metric

At-home practice Coach Buddy assigns in recap via `recommend_drill`. Each
entry maps to a **live scorecard metric** and includes reps, fix cue, and
safety notes. All drills are **off-ice** (garage, basement, driveway).

**Tags:** RUBRIC, OFF-ICE  
**Source:** Buddy Live coaching rubric aligned with `metrics-*.md` and
`app/tools/coaching.py` `_DRILL_RECOMMENDATIONS`.

## Wristshot metrics

### front knee bend

**Homework:** 3 sets of 10 shadow wrist shots. Pause at the lowest knee
bend and hold one second before release.  
**Fix cue:** Get lower — imagine sitting back into a chair before the release.  
**Search hint:** USA Hockey knee bend shooting drill off-ice.

### weight transfer

**Homework:** Wall-shoot line: tape an X on the floor — back foot on one
mark, finish on the front mark every rep. 20 reps slow.  
**Fix cue:** Drive weight from back foot to front foot through the puck.  
**Search hint:** hockey weight transfer off-ice drill.

### back leg push

**Homework:** 15 reps emphasizing a straight back leg at release — say
"push" out loud when the back leg extends.  
**Fix cue:** Snap the back leg straight like a coiled spring.  
**Search hint:** hockey back leg push drill.

### bottom hand

**Homework:** 20 bottom-hand-only pulls — top hand loose on the stick,
pull bottom hand to hip on each rep.  
**Fix cue:** Pull hard with the bottom hand toward the hip.  
**Search hint:** hockey bottom hand shooting drill.

### top hand

**Homework:** 20 reps watching only the top wrist — exaggerate the roll
over at the end.  
**Fix cue:** Roll the top wrist over at the very end — that's the snap.  
**Search hint:** hockey top hand wrist snap drill.

### puck position at contact

**Homework:** Place a shoe as "front foot marker." Puck must start at
back foot and contact beside the marker. 15 reps.  
**Fix cue:** Contact off the front foot, not the back foot.  
**Search hint:** hockey puck contact point drill.

### stick flex

**Homework:** 15 "load only" reps — push stick into the floor without
releasing, feel the bend, then reset.  
**Fix cue:** Push down into the floor to load the stick before the snap.  
**Search hint:** hockey stick flex loading drill.

### stance

**Homework:** Athletic stance hold — 30 seconds on, 15 off, 4 rounds
before any shots.  
**Fix cue:** Shoulder-width feet, knees bent, head over the puck.  
**Search hint:** hockey athletic stance drill.

## Slapshot form metrics

### stance and base

**Homework:** Wider stance shadow slap shots — feet past shoulder width,
10 slow reps checking base before wind-up.  
**Fix cue:** Feet wider than shoulders, weight on back leg before wind-up.

### wind up

**Homework:** 15 wind-ups stopping at waist height — no over-the-head
swing.  
**Fix cue:** Stick to waist height — short and snappy.

### front knee bend at impact

**Homework:** 10 reps pausing at "stick on floor" with front knee bent.  
**Fix cue:** Bend the front knee as the stick hits the floor.

### power sequence

**Homework:** Verbal chain drill — say "hips, shoulders, arms" on each
slow rep. 12 reps.  
**Fix cue:** Hips first, then shoulders, then arms.

### stick mechanics

**Homework:** Mark a line an inch behind a ball/puck; stick must hit the
line before contact. 12 reps.  
**Fix cue:** Hit the floor behind the puck so the stick flexes.

### follow through

**Homework:** 15 reps pointing blade at a target on the wall after release.  
**Fix cue:** Point the blade where you want the puck to go.

## Backhand metrics

### posture and balance

**Homework:** 12 slow shadow backhands — stay tall, no lean back at release.  
**Fix cue:** Stay tall through the shot.

### extension through release

**Homework:** 15 reps exaggerating hands finishing high across the body.  
**Fix cue:** Push hands up and across after release.

### puck control roll

**Homework:** Cup-and-roll with a ball — roll from heel to toe of blade
10 times each side.  
**Fix cue:** Roll the puck from back of blade to front.

### top hand control

**Homework:** Top hand extended away from body on 15 slow reps — check
gap at armpit.  
**Fix cue:** Top hand away from the body so the stick travels freely.

### blade angle

**Homework:** 12 reps with a closed blade cue — "cup the puck" before
release.  
**Fix cue:** Closed blade lifts the puck for upper corners.

## Daily consistency fallback

When no metric stands out or grounding misses:

**Homework:** 50 wristshots a day for one week — 25 slow (form), 25 game
speed. Same time, same spot, track one cue per day.  
**Fix cue:** Consistency beats cramming — same cue all week.
