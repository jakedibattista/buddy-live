# Hockey IQ — USA Hockey Angling and Stick Checks

Learn how to separate an opponent from the puck safely and cleanly, using USA Hockey ADM positioning concepts. This is how you win the puck back without taking penalties.

## What is angling?

Angling means skating at an angle to force the player with the puck toward the boards. Instead of skating straight at them, you shadow them from the inside out. This takes away their skating room and forces them to go where you want them to go.

Common kid scenario: an opponent is skating fast down the wing. If you skate straight at them, they'll deke right past you. 
* **The fix:** skate on an angle toward a spot ahead of them along the boards. Keep your body on their inside shoulder. This forces them to run out of room against the boards!

## The stick check

Once you've angled the player to the boards and there's no space left, it's time to check their stick, not their body. USA Hockey teaches you to "stick on puck, body on body."

* **Stick on puck:** keep your stick blade low on the ice. Aim your stick blade directly at their puck. 
* **The lift check:** slide your stick under theirs and lift their stick up fast. The puck will slide right out, and now it's yours!
* **No slashing:** never swing your stick down or stick it between their legs. Keep it controlled and lift from underneath.

## Angling checklist

1. **Inside out:** stay between the puck carrier and the middle of the ice. Force them wide.
2. **Watch the chest:** don't watch their puck or head—watch their chest! Players can deke with their heads or sticks, but their chest tells you exactly where they are skating.
3. **Stick on puck:** keep your stick low to cut off passing lanes as you close the distance.
