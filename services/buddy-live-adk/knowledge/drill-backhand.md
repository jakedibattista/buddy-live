# Backhand

The sneaky shot. The puck starts off the back heel of the blade. Sweep
through with the bottom of the blade cupping the puck, roll the wrists,
and follow through high and across the body. Hard to read because
goalies expect the forehand — the backhand is gold in tight or on a
breakaway.

## Key technique points

1. **Puck starting position:** off the back heel of the blade — not in
   the middle.
2. **Posture and balance:** stay tall through the shot — don't lean
   back as you release.
3. **Top hand control:** keep the top hand away from the body so the
   stick can travel freely.
4. **Bottom hand:** drive forward through the puck.
5. **Blade angle:** cup the puck on release. A closed blade lifts it
   for the upper corners.
6. **Puck control roll:** roll the puck from the back of the blade to
   the front as you sweep through.
7. **Extension through release:** push the hands up and across the body
   after release — full extension.

## Kid-friendly cheat sheet (10–13 yo)

"Puck starts on the back heel of your blade. Stand tall. Keep your top
hand away from your body. Cup the puck and sweep across, then push your
hands up and across after the puck leaves — like you're reaching toward
the top corner."

## Common mistakes

- Puck too far forward at the start → can't get the roll, the puck
  squibs.
- Top hand glued to the body → stick can't travel, no power.
- Leaning back → shot lifts too much, no accuracy.
- Open blade at release → puck flutters, no zip.

## Recommended drill: cup-and-roll backhands

5 reps slow, focusing on the puck rolling heel-to-toe along the blade.
Then 5 reps at full speed aiming for the top of a target. Search hint:
"hockey backhand cup roll drill."
