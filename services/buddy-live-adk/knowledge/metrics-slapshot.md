# Slapshot scoring metrics

Eight metrics scored on every slapshot rep. All 0–10; 7+ is good.

## stance and base

**What it means:** wider stance than the wristshot, weight on the back
leg before the wind-up. Sets up the whole shot.

**Good looks like:** feet wider than the shoulders, clear lean back
onto the back leg before the swing.

**Fix cue:** "Feet wider than your shoulders, weight on the back leg
before the wind-up."

**Recommended drill:** slapshot stance drill. Hold the base before
shooting. Search hint: "hockey slap shot stance drill."

## wind up

**What it means:** how high the stick comes back. Waist height is ideal
— over the head is too slow and telegraphs the shot.

**Good looks like:** stick back to waist height, no higher.

**Fix cue:** "Stick to waist height, not over your head — short and
snappy."

**Recommended drill:** slapshot wind-up drill. Practice the short wind-up
without a puck. Search hint: "hockey slap shot wind up drill."

## front knee bend at impact

**What it means:** bending the front knee as the stick hits the floor.
Locks the leg drive into the shot.

**Good looks like:** front knee clearly bent at the moment the stick
hits the floor.

**Fix cue:** "Bend your front knee as the stick hits the floor — drive
through it."

**Recommended drill:** slapshot knee bend drill. Search hint: "hockey
slap shot knee bend drill."

## weight transfer

**What it means:** shifting weight from back foot to front foot through
contact. Same idea as the wristshot — power from the legs.

**Good looks like:** weight clearly finishes on the front foot at
follow-through.

**Fix cue:** "Drive your weight from back foot to front foot through
the puck."

**Recommended drill:** wall-shoot weight-transfer drill. Search hint:
"hockey weight transfer drill."

## power sequence

**What it means:** the kinetic chain — hips first, then shoulders,
then arms. Wrong order = arms-only shot.

**Good looks like:** hips visibly rotate first, shoulders follow, arms
finish.

**Fix cue:** "Hips first, then shoulders, then arms — chain it together."

**Recommended drill:** slapshot kinetic-chain drill. Practice with hand
on hip to feel the rotation. Search hint: "hockey slap shot power
sequence drill."

## stick mechanics

**What it means:** hitting the floor about an inch behind the puck so
the stick flexes into the shot. The flex is the engine.

**Good looks like:** visible stick flex before the puck releases.

**Fix cue:** "Hit the floor an inch behind the puck so the stick flexes
into the shot."

**Recommended drill:** slapshot stick flex drill. Use a tape mark on
the floor as a target an inch behind the puck. Search hint: "hockey
slap shot stick flex drill."

## follow through

**What it means:** where the blade points after release. Good follow-
through = good accuracy.

**Good looks like:** blade pointing at the target after the puck leaves.

**Fix cue:** "Point the blade where you want the puck to go after
release."

**Recommended drill:** slapshot follow-through drill. Aim at corners
of a target. Search hint: "hockey slap shot follow through drill."

## arm mechanics

**What it means:** keeping the top arm straighter through the swing —
don't collapse the elbow.

**Good looks like:** top arm extended through the swing, no early
elbow bend.

**Fix cue:** "Keep your top arm straighter through the swing — don't
collapse the elbow."

**Recommended drill:** slapshot arm extension drill. Search hint:
"hockey slap shot arm extension drill."
