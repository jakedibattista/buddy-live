# Slapshot

The power shot. The stick comes up to about waist height, drives down
into the floor about an inch behind the puck so the stick flexes, then
the flex releases through the puck. Slapshots take longer to release
than wristshots but generate way more speed — used from distance.

## Key technique points

1. **Stance and base:** feet wider than the shoulders, weight on the
   back leg before the wind-up.
2. **Wind up:** stick comes back to about waist height — short and
   snappy. Over the head is too slow and tips the goalie off.
3. **Strike point:** hit the floor about an inch behind the puck so the
   stick flexes into the shot. Don't hit the puck directly first.
4. **Front knee bend at impact:** bend the front knee as the stick hits
   the floor — drive through it.
5. **Power sequence:** hips first, then shoulders, then arms. Chain the
   body together.
6. **Stick flex:** the stick should visibly bend before the puck releases.
7. **Follow through:** point the blade where you want the puck to go.
8. **Arm mechanics:** keep the top arm straighter through the swing —
   don't collapse the elbow.

## Kid-friendly cheat sheet (10–13 yo)

"Feet wide. Weight on the back foot. Bring the stick up to your waist —
not over your head. Hit the floor an inch behind the puck so the stick
bends. Drive through your front knee. Point the blade where you want
the puck to go."

## Common mistakes

- Windup over the head → slow release, telegraphs the shot.
- Hitting the puck directly (no stick flex) → arms-only power, no zip.
- Standing tall through impact → no leg drive, weak shot.
- Hips locked → upper-body-only swing, no chain.

## Recommended drill: stick-flex slapshot

5 slow reps focusing on hitting an inch behind the puck and watching the
stick bend. Then 5 reps at full speed. Search hint: "hockey slap shot
stick flex drill."
