# Hockey IQ — scenario catalog (structured)

Ready-to-run scenarios for IQ Coach and inline analysis-wait questions.
Each entry has spoken scenario, options, correct answer, coach reaction,
and diagram hints for `show_iq_visual`.

**Tags:** IQ-CARD, ADM  
**Vary order every session** — do not read top-to-bottom. Pull different
sections via `lookup_drill_knowledge("hockey IQ scenario breakaway ages 11")`.

## Rules — goal on the line (ages 8–12)

**Scenario:** The puck is on the goal line. Half of it crossed, half didn't. Is it a goal?  
**Options:** Goal | No goal  
**Correct:** No goal  
**Coach line:** The whole puck has to cross — half on the line doesn't count.  
**Diagram:** puck on the goal line in front of the net

## Rules — offside rush (ages 9–13)

**Scenario:** Your teammate skated into their end before the puck crossed the blue line. What's the call?  
**Options:** Offside | Icing  
**Correct:** Offside  
**Coach line:** The puck has to cross first — that's offside.  
**Diagram:** player at the blue line, puck behind

## Rules — icing clear (ages 9–13)

**Scenario:** You're in your own end and slap the puck all the way down. Nobody touches it. What happens?  
**Options:** Icing | Offside  
**Correct:** Icing  
**Coach line:** That's icing — play stops and the faceoff comes back.  
**Diagram:** player at the blue line shooting down the ice

## Breakaway — goalie way out (ages 8–12, wristshot)

**Scenario:** Breakaway. The goalie is way out in front of the net. Shoot fast or skate around?  
**Options:** Shoot fast | Skate around  
**Correct:** Skate around  
**Coach line:** When they're way out, going around opens more net.  
**Diagram:** on a breakaway, goalie way out

## Breakaway — goalie square (ages 11–14)

**Scenario:** Breakaway. Goalie is square and set. High glove or five-hole?  
**Options:** High glove | Five-hole  
**Correct:** High glove  
**Coach line:** Square goalie — pick a corner, high glove is a classic finish.  
**Diagram:** on a breakaway, goalie square

## Slot — defender sliding (ages 10–13)

**Scenario:** You catch a pass in the slot. A defender is sliding toward you. Quick shot or wait?  
**Options:** Quick shot | Wait for a better look  
**Correct:** Quick shot  
**Coach line:** Shoot before the lane closes — that's the high-percentage play.  
**Diagram:** you have the puck right in front of the net, defender sliding from the right circle

## 2-on-1 — defender takes pass (ages 11–14)

**Scenario:** Two-on-one. The defender slides to your teammate. What do you do?  
**Options:** Pass | Shoot  
**Correct:** Shoot  
**Coach line:** They gave you the shot — take it.  
**Diagram:** 2-on-1, defender closing, teammate in the slot

## 2-on-1 — saucer or shoot (ages 13+, backhand)

**Scenario:** Two-on-one rush. Defender has stick in the pass lane. Saucer pass or shoot?  
**Options:** Saucer pass | Shoot  
**Correct:** Shoot  
**Coach line:** If the pass lane is blocked, shoot — don't force a fancy play.  
**Diagram:** 2-on-1, defender closing from the right circle

## Point shot — screen or tip (ages 12–14)

**Scenario:** Your defenseman has a shot from the point. You're in front of the net. Screen or tip?  
**Options:** Stand still in the lane | Move to tip or screen  
**Correct:** Move to tip or screen  
**Coach line:** Get active — screens and tips beat a clear goalie view.  
**Diagram:** teammate at the blue line, you in the slot, goalie square

## Forecheck — F1 role (ages 11–13)

**Scenario:** The other team has the puck in their corner. You're the closest forward. Chase the puck or cover the pass?  
**Options:** Chase the puck | Take away the pass  
**Correct:** Chase the puck  
**Coach line:** You're F1 — pressure the puck carrier first.  
**Diagram:** in the left corner, defender with puck

## Backcheck — pick a man (ages 11–14)

**Scenario:** The other team is rushing. Three attackers, you're backchecking. Who do you pick up?  
**Options:** The puck carrier only | The open attacker without the puck  
**Correct:** The open attacker without the puck  
**Coach line:** Someone has to cover the trailer — don't all chase the puck.  
**Diagram:** defender sliding from the right circle, player in the slot

## Power play — quick puck movement (ages 12–14)

**Scenario:** Power play. You have an open seam but a teammate has a better one-timer angle. Hold or move it?  
**Options:** Hold for your shot | Move it quickly  
**Correct:** Move it quickly  
**Coach line:** On the PP, move the puck — find the best look.  
**Diagram:** teammate in the slot, goalie moving across

## Penalty kill — clear or ice (ages 10–13)

**Scenario:** Penalty kill. You're under pressure in your end. Clear it hard or try to stickhandle out?  
**Options:** Clear it hard | Stickhandle through  
**Correct:** Clear it hard  
**Coach line:** PK job one — get it out, kill time.  
**Diagram:** player at the blue line, pressure from the corner

## Late game — up one (ages 11–14)

**Scenario:** You're up by one with two minutes left. Force a risky pass or chip it safe?  
**Options:** Force the pass | Chip it safe  
**Correct:** Chip it safe  
**Coach line:** Protect the lead — no hero plays.  
**Diagram:** at the blue line, defender closing

## Late game — down one (ages 11–14)

**Scenario:** Down by one, one minute left. Pull the goalie for an extra attacker?  
**Options:** Yes, pull the goalie | No, keep the goalie in  
**Correct:** Yes, pull the goalie  
**Coach line:** You need a goal — extra attacker is worth the risk.  
**Diagram:** empty net situation, player in the slot

## Small area — give and go (ages 9–12, ADM)

**Scenario:** Tight space. Your teammate has the puck and you're beside them. Pass and go or stand still?  
**Options:** Pass and sprint for it back | Stand still  
**Correct:** Pass and sprint for it back  
**Coach line:** Give-and-go — move right after the pass.  
**Diagram:** in the left corner, teammate with puck

## Angling — straight at or angle (ages 11–14, ADM)

**Scenario:** Opponent rushing down the wing. Skate straight at them or angle them to the boards?  
**Options:** Straight at them | Angle to the boards  
**Correct:** Angle to the boards  
**Coach line:** Angle them wide — take away the middle.  
**Diagram:** defender sliding from the right circle, player on the wing

## Puck protection — boards (ages 10–13, ADM)

**Scenario:** Defender is on you along the boards. Turn your back to protect or turn toward them?  
**Options:** Back to defender, puck outside | Face the defender  
**Correct:** Back to defender, puck outside  
**Coach line:** Butt to defender, puck on the outside — body shield.  
**Diagram:** in the left corner, defender closing

## Slapshot choice — time and space (ages 10–13)

**Scenario:** You have the puck at the point with a defender closing fast. Big windup or quick snap?  
**Options:** Big windup | Quick snap  
**Correct:** Quick snap  
**Coach line:** No time — snap it before the block.  
**Diagram:** at the blue line, defender closing

## Wristshot — hash marks decision (ages 13+)

**Scenario:** You enter the zone at the hash marks. Defender gap is big. Pass or drive wide?  
**Options:** Pass across | Drive wide  
**Correct:** Drive wide  
**Coach line:** Big gap — attack the space, make the goalie move.  
**Diagram:** at the left circle, defender closing
