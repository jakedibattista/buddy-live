# Hockey IQ — rules basics

Quick-reference rules explanations for the IQ Coach. Kid-level wording.
Each rule has a short explanation, a common scenario, and the right call.

## Scoring a goal

The **whole puck** has to cross the goal line. If half is on the line
and half is over — no goal. If all of it crosses, even just barely —
goal counts.

Common kid scenario: a goalie is sitting on the puck and the puck is
halfway across the line. **No goal.** It has to be fully across.

## Offside

You can't be in the other team's end before the puck. Two of your
skates have to be on or behind the blue line until the puck crosses
first.

Common scenario: a teammate skates into the offensive zone and the
puck arrives a half-second later. **Offside.** The puck has to cross
the line before any teammate does.

## Icing

You can't shoot the puck from your own half all the way across the
opponent's goal line without a teammate touching it first. It stops
play.

Common scenario: defender is under pressure, slaps the puck all the
way down the ice to clear it. If no one touches it, **icing** — play
stops and the faceoff comes back to the defender's end.

## Penalty

When a player breaks a rule (tripping, slashing, hooking, etc.) they
go to the penalty box for two minutes. Their team plays a player
short — that's the "power play" for the other team.

Common scenario: a defender slashes an attacker's stick to stop a
shot. **Penalty.** The defender sits, the offense gets a power play.

## Power play vs penalty kill

- **Power play (PP):** your team has more skaters than the other team
  because they took a penalty. Big chance to score.
- **Penalty kill (PK):** your team has fewer skaters because YOU took a
  penalty. Job is to defend hard and kill the clock until you're back
  to full strength.

## Faceoff

Play restarts at a faceoff dot. The two centers line up, the ref drops
the puck, both fight to win it back to their team.

## Pulling the goalie

Late in the game, if your team is losing by one or two, the coach can
take the goalie off the ice and put an extra skater on. It's all-or-
nothing — score the tying goal or risk an empty-net goal against.

Common scenario: team is down by one with one minute left. **Yes, pull
the goalie.** It's worth the risk.
